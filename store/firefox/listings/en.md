# AdBlocker

Free ad blocking with site controls, live counters and no browsing-data collection.

AdBlocker filters listed advertising and tracking requests and marked ad placements locally. Use global, site and exact-URL controls, a one-time pause, separate request/placement counters, and light/dark themes. English is the default; choose another interface language in the popup. Every blocking feature is free, without an account or subscription. Support is voluntary. Settings and selected exceptions stay in browser-local storage; browsing history and form contents are not collected. Store updates are managed by the browser. Coverage is targeted, not universal; some ads may remain and exceptions may be needed. Protection changes reload the page automatically. Pause expiry is asynchronous. Private browsing is not supported.
